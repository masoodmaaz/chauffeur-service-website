About Elite Chauffeur

Delivering Excellence in Luxury Transportation Since 2010

Our Story

Elite Chauffeur was founded in 2010 with a simple mission: to provide the highest quality chauffeur services in the region. What started as a small operation has grown into a trusted name in luxury transportation. We believe that transportation is more than just getting from point A to point B—it's about the experience.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional, reliable, and luxurious chauffeur services that exceed our clients' expectations. We are committed to punctuality, professionalism, and personalized service for every journey.

Our Team

Our chauffeurs are carefully selected and extensively trained professionals. Each team member is:

  • Background checked and fully vetted
  • Trained in defensive driving and safety protocols
  • Knowledgeable about local routes and destinations
  • Professional, courteous, and customer-focused
  • Experienced in handling diverse client needs

Our Fleet

We maintain a modern fleet of luxury vehicles, all meticulously maintained and regularly serviced. Our vehicles include:

  • Executive Sedans
  • Luxury SUVs
  • Limousines
  • Premium Vans for group travel
